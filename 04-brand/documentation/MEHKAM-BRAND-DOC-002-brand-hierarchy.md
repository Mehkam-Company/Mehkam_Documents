# Brand Hierarchy

<div align="center">
  <img src="../assets/logo/png/mehkam-symbol-white-2048w.png" alt="Mehkam Logo" width="150" />
</div>

## Overview

This document defines the official brand hierarchy of Mehkam Holding.

The purpose of the hierarchy is to establish a clear relationship between Mehkam Holding, its portfolio companies, subsidiary brands, and future brands within the Mehkam ecosystem.

The hierarchy provides a structural model for understanding brand ownership, brand relationships, organizational identity, and the relationship between the Holding brand and independently operated businesses.

The Brand Hierarchy is designed to support Mehkam's long-term growth while preserving both Holding-level identity and Business Unit independence.

# Purpose

The purpose of the Mehkam Brand Hierarchy is to:

- Define the relationship between Mehkam Holding and its businesses.
- Establish a clear brand ownership structure.
- Provide a scalable model for future companies and brands.
- Prevent ambiguity between Holding-level and Business Unit-level identities.
- Support consistent brand governance.
- Preserve independent positioning for portfolio companies.
- Establish a foundation for future brand architecture decisions.

# 1. Brand Hierarchy Model

Mehkam follows a parent-to-business brand hierarchy.

The fundamental structure is:

```text
Mehkam Holding
│
├── Portfolio Company A
│   └── Products / Services
│
├── Portfolio Company B
│   └── Products / Services
│
├── Portfolio Company C
│   └── Products / Services
│
└── Future Portfolio Companies
    └── Products / Services
```

Mehkam Holding is the parent organization and ultimate owner of the businesses within its ecosystem.

Each portfolio company may maintain an independent brand identity and market position while remaining part of the Mehkam ecosystem.

# 2. Holding Brand

## Mehkam Holding

Mehkam Holding represents the parent organization responsible for:

- Ownership.
- Strategic direction.
- Portfolio management.
- Governance.
- Long-term value creation.
- Ecosystem development.

The Mehkam Holding brand represents the organizational and strategic identity of the overall ecosystem.

It should not unnecessarily replace the identities of independent portfolio companies.

# 3. Portfolio Company Brands

Portfolio companies operate as independent businesses within the Mehkam ecosystem.

Each company may maintain its own:

- Brand name.
- Visual identity.
- Positioning.
- Market strategy.
- Communication system.
- Product identity.
- Customer experience.

Portfolio companies remain connected to Mehkam through ownership and organizational structure rather than requiring identical market-facing identities.

The relationship can be represented as:

```text
Mehkam Holding
        │
        ├── Ownership
        ├── Governance
        ├── Strategic Direction
        └── Shared Capabilities
                │
                ▼
        Portfolio Company
                │
                ├── Brand Identity
                ├── Product
                ├── Market
                └── Customers
```

# 4. Product and Service Brands

A portfolio company may create multiple products or services.

Products and services may maintain their own identities when there is a clear strategic or commercial reason.

The structure may therefore become:

```text
Mehkam Holding
│
└── Portfolio Company
    │
    ├── Product A
    │
    ├── Product B
    │
    └── Product C
```

Product-level branding must remain consistent with the parent company's strategic and brand architecture decisions.

# 5. Brand Relationship Models

Mehkam recognizes three primary relationship levels.

## 5.1 Holding Brand

The Holding brand represents Mehkam's organizational identity.

```text
Mehkam Holding
```

It communicates:

- Ownership.
- Ecosystem identity.
- Strategic leadership.
- Organizational credibility.

## 5.2 Independent Business Brand

An independent business operates under its own market-facing identity.

```text
Mehkam Holding
        ↓
Portfolio Company
        ↓
Independent Brand
```

The business may operate independently while remaining owned and governed by Mehkam.

## 5.3 Product Brand

A product may operate under a portfolio company while maintaining a distinct product identity.

```text
Mehkam Holding
        ↓
Portfolio Company
        ↓
Product Brand
```

The appropriate relationship should be determined by business strategy, market requirements, and brand governance.

# 6. Brand Independence

Mehkam's brand hierarchy supports business independence.

Portfolio companies are not required to use identical:

- Visual systems.
- Messaging.
- Brand positioning.
- Customer communication.
- Product identities.

Independence allows each business to compete effectively within its own market.

However, brand independence does not remove:

- Ownership relationships.
- Governance responsibilities.
- Strategic alignment.
- Documentation requirements.

# 7. Brand Association

Portfolio companies may communicate their relationship with Mehkam when strategically appropriate.

Possible association models include:

```text
Owned by Mehkam Holding
```

or:

```text
A Mehkam Holding Company
```

The exact public-facing relationship should be determined according to the company's brand strategy and applicable brand guidelines.

Brand association should create credibility and ecosystem recognition without weakening the independent identity of the portfolio company.

# 8. Brand Architecture Principles

## 8.1 Clear Ownership

Every brand within the Mehkam ecosystem must have clearly defined ownership.

## 8.2 Independent Market Identity

Portfolio companies should maintain independent market identities when independence creates strategic value.

## 8.3 Strategic Alignment

Every portfolio brand should remain aligned with Mehkam's broader strategic direction.

## 8.4 Scalable Structure

The hierarchy must support the addition of new companies, sub-brands, products, and services.

## 8.5 Avoid Unnecessary Complexity

New brand layers should only be introduced when they create meaningful strategic or commercial value.

## 8.6 Consistent Governance

All brands remain subject to applicable Mehkam governance and brand documentation standards.

# 9. Brand Creation and Integration

When Mehkam creates or acquires a new business, the brand relationship should be evaluated before the business is formally integrated into the ecosystem.

The process includes:

```text
Business Creation / Acquisition
        ↓
Brand Evaluation
        ↓
Ownership Definition
        ↓
Brand Relationship Definition
        ↓
Brand Architecture Decision
        ↓
Documentation
        ↓
Brand Implementation
```

The resulting relationship must be documented in the appropriate brand documentation.

# 10. Brand Architecture Decision Criteria

Brand hierarchy decisions should consider:

- Business independence.
- Target market.
- Customer perception.
- Competitive positioning.
- Strategic value.
- Portfolio relationship.
- Brand equity.
- Operational complexity.
- Long-term scalability.

A new brand layer should not be created solely for organizational convenience.

# 11. Responsibilities

## Mehkam Holding

Mehkam Holding is responsible for:

- Defining Holding-level brand architecture.
- Protecting the Mehkam brand.
- Approving major ecosystem-level brand changes.
- Maintaining ownership clarity.

## Mehkam Brand Team

The Mehkam Brand Team is responsible for:

- Maintaining the brand hierarchy.
- Reviewing brand architecture decisions.
- Maintaining documentation.
- Establishing applicable brand standards.
- Supporting portfolio companies.

## Portfolio Companies

Portfolio companies are responsible for:

- Maintaining their own brand systems.
- Following applicable brand standards.
- Maintaining accurate brand documentation.
- Protecting their market identity.
- Reporting major brand changes when required.

# 12. Governance

The Brand Hierarchy is an official Mehkam Holding brand framework.

Changes to the following require appropriate governance review:

- Holding-level brand structure.
- Portfolio company relationships.
- Major sub-brand structures.
- Ownership representation.
- Brand architecture principles.

Routine brand execution remains the responsibility of the relevant Business Unit.

Governance should protect brand consistency and ownership clarity without creating unnecessary operational bottlenecks.

# 13. Review

The Brand Hierarchy should be reviewed:

- When a new portfolio company is created.
- When a new company is acquired.
- When a major sub-brand is introduced.
- When ownership structures change.
- When brand architecture materially changes.
- During scheduled brand strategy reviews.

The document should remain aligned with the current Mehkam organizational and portfolio structure.

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Mehkam Holding.
- Current portfolio companies.
- Future portfolio companies.
- Subsidiary brands.
- Sub-brands.
- Product brands.
- Brand and marketing teams.
- Business Unit leadership.

# Ownership

**Owner:** Mehkam Holding

**Responsible Team:** Mehkam Brand Team

The Mehkam Brand Team is responsible for maintaining the hierarchy and ensuring that changes remain consistent with Mehkam's strategic and governance requirements.

# Final Statement

The Mehkam Brand Hierarchy establishes the structural relationship between Mehkam Holding, its portfolio companies, subsidiary brands, and product-level identities.

The hierarchy is designed to combine **clear Holding-level ownership with independent business-level brand execution**.

This structure enables Mehkam to build a scalable ecosystem of independent companies while maintaining organizational clarity, strategic alignment, brand governance, and long-term brand value.
