# Brand Asset Governance

---

<div align="center">
  <img src="../../assets/logo/mehkam-symbol.svg" alt="Mehkam Logo" width="150" />
</div>

## Overview

This document defines the official governance framework for all brand assets owned, created, managed, or distributed within the Mehkam Holding ecosystem.

It establishes the rules for:

- Asset ownership
- Asset identification
- Naming
- Versioning
- Approval
- Publication
- Maintenance
- Retirement
- Archival

The objective is to ensure that every Mehkam brand asset remains controlled, traceable, consistent, and aligned with the official Brand System.

---

# 1. Governance Principles

All Mehkam brand assets are governed according to the following principles:

1. **Centralized Ownership**
2. **Standardized Identification**
3. **Controlled Versioning**
4. **Centralized Approval**
5. **Traceability**
6. **Lifecycle Management**
7. **Brand Consistency**
8. **Controlled Distribution**

No brand asset should become an official Mehkam asset without entering the defined governance process.

---

# 2. Asset Ownership

## 2.1 Ownership Model

All Mehkam brand assets are centrally owned by:

> **Mehkam Holding**

This includes assets created internally, externally, collaboratively, or through third-party production processes when those assets are formally adopted into the Mehkam Brand System.

---

## 2.2 Ownership Scope

Central ownership applies to all official brand asset categories, including but not limited to:

- Logos
- Logo variations
- Icons
- Typography assets
- Color assets
- Templates
- Social media assets
- Presentation assets
- Marketing assets
- Corporate communication assets
- Photography
- Illustrations
- Digital graphics
- Brand documents
- Campaign assets
- Master design files

---

## 2.3 Production vs. Ownership

The creator or production team of an asset does not automatically become its owner.

Production responsibility and ownership are separate concepts.

A team, designer, contractor, agency, or individual may produce an asset while Mehkam Holding remains the official owner.

---

# 3. Asset Identification

Every official brand asset must be identifiable through the centralized Mehkam Asset Governance System.

Each asset must have sufficient metadata to establish:

- Asset identity
- Asset category
- Version
- Status
- Ownership
- Production context
- Approval state
- Lifecycle state

The identification system must allow assets to be traced throughout their lifecycle.

---

# 4. Naming Standard

## 4.1 Centralized Naming

All Mehkam brand assets must follow a centralized and mandatory naming standard.

Ad-hoc naming is not permitted for official assets.

The naming system must be:

- Consistent
- Predictable
- Machine-readable where applicable
- Human-readable
- Searchable
- Scalable

---

## 4.2 Naming Structure

The exact naming convention must be defined by the Mehkam Brand Asset Naming Standard.

At minimum, the naming system should allow identification of:

- Brand
- Asset domain
- Asset type
- Asset purpose
- Variant where applicable
- Version where applicable

Example structure:

```text
MEHKAM-[DOMAIN]-[ASSET-TYPE]-[PURPOSE]-[VARIANT]-v[VERSION]
```

Example:

```text
MEHKAM-BRAND-LOGO-PRIMARY-BLACK-v1.0.0
```

The exact implementation may vary according to the technical requirements of each asset category, but deviation from the centralized standard requires governance approval.

---

# 5. Versioning Standard

## 5.1 Mandatory Versioning

All version-controlled brand assets must use the centralized Mehkam versioning standard.

Versions must be explicit and traceable.

---

## 5.2 Version Integrity

A new version must be created when an approved asset undergoes a meaningful modification.

Version changes must never overwrite historical versions without preserving the previous state.

The purpose of versioning is to maintain:

- Change traceability
- Historical integrity
- Reproducibility
- Rollback capability
- Governance visibility

---

## 5.3 Master Assets

Master assets must maintain their version history.

Derived formats or exports should reference the applicable master version whenever technically possible.

The master version remains the authoritative source for controlled assets.

---

# 6. Approval Workflow

## 6.1 Centralized Approval

All official Mehkam brand assets require centralized approval by:

> **Mehkam Brand Governance**

before publication or official distribution.

No asset may be considered an official Mehkam brand asset solely because it has been created or completed.

---

## 6.2 Approval Sequence

The standard approval workflow is:

```text
Production
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Approval
    ↓
Publication
```

Each stage must be completed before the asset progresses to the next stage.

---

## 6.3 Review

The review stage evaluates whether the asset satisfies the applicable Mehkam standards.

Depending on the asset type, review may include:

- Visual Identity compliance
- Logo compliance
- Typography compliance
- Color compliance
- Composition compliance
- Technical requirements
- Naming compliance
- Versioning compliance
- Brand consistency
- Legal or usage constraints where applicable

---

## 6.4 Approval Criteria

An asset should only receive approval when:

1. It complies with the applicable Brand Standards.
2. It follows the required naming convention.
3. It has the correct version information.
4. It satisfies technical requirements.
5. Its intended usage is defined.
6. Its ownership is established.
7. Its lifecycle state is correctly recorded.

---

## 6.5 Publication

Publication is only permitted after formal approval.

Approved assets may then be distributed through the appropriate official channels.

Unapproved assets must not be presented as official Mehkam brand assets.

---

# 7. Asset Lifecycle

Every official asset follows a controlled lifecycle:

```text
Production
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Approval
    ↓
Publication
    ↓
Maintenance
    ↓
Retirement
```

Each lifecycle state has a distinct purpose.

---

## 7.1 Production

The asset is being created or prepared.

At this stage:

- The asset is not official.
- Publication is not permitted.
- Iteration is expected.
- The applicable standards should already be considered.

---

## 7.2 Review

The asset is being evaluated against applicable standards.

The asset may be modified based on review findings.

The asset remains non-published until approval is granted.

---

## 7.3 Approval

The asset has successfully passed the required governance review.

Approval establishes the asset as an authorized candidate for publication.

---

## 7.4 Publication

The approved asset is officially released for its intended use.

Published assets become part of the active Mehkam Brand Asset Library.

---

## 7.5 Maintenance

Published assets may require controlled maintenance.

Maintenance may include:

- Format updates
- Technical corrections
- Compatibility changes
- Controlled revisions
- New approved variants
- Corrections required by updated Brand Standards

Meaningful changes require appropriate version updates and may require re-approval.

---

## 7.6 Retirement

An asset enters retirement when it is no longer approved for active use.

Retirement may occur because of:

- Brand evolution
- Asset replacement
- Technical obsolescence
- Strategic change
- Updated Brand Standards
- Superseding versions

Retired assets must no longer be used for new official communications.

---

# 8. Archived Assets

## 8.1 Archive Requirement

Retired assets must be transferred to the official Mehkam archive.

They must be retained for historical and governance records.

---

## 8.2 Archive Integrity

Archived assets should preserve their relevant metadata, including:

- Original identity
- Version
- Ownership
- Approval history
- Publication context
- Retirement state
- Retirement date where applicable

Archived assets must remain distinguishable from active assets.

---

## 8.3 Archive Usage

Archived assets are maintained for:

- Historical reference
- Governance records
- Documentation
- Auditability
- Brand evolution analysis
- Legal or organizational records where applicable

Archived assets must not be treated as active assets.

---

# 9. Asset Status Model

Asset lifecycle and operational status must remain distinguishable.

The lifecycle model is:

| Lifecycle State | Description                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Production      | Asset is being created                            |
| Review          | Asset is under governance evaluation              |
| Approval        | Asset has passed formal approval                  |
| Publication     | Asset is officially active                        |
| Maintenance     | Published asset is being maintained               |
| Retirement      | Asset is no longer approved for active use        |
| Archive         | Retired asset is preserved for historical records |

The Archive state represents preservation after retirement rather than active brand usage.

---

# 10. Asset Library Rules

The official Asset Library must distinguish between:

- Active assets
- Assets under production
- Assets under review
- Approved assets awaiting publication
- Retired assets
- Archived assets

Only approved and published assets should be considered authoritative for active use.

Teams must not use obsolete, unapproved, or retired assets when an approved active alternative exists.

---

# 11. Governance Responsibilities

## 11.1 Mehkam Holding

Mehkam Holding is responsible for:

- Asset ownership
- Brand governance
- Governance standards
- Approval authority
- Lifecycle policy
- Asset integrity
- Brand consistency

---

## 11.2 Asset Producers

Designers, engineers, agencies, contractors, or internal teams producing assets are responsible for:

- Following applicable standards
- Using the correct naming convention
- Maintaining required metadata
- Preparing assets for review
- Addressing review feedback
- Maintaining production integrity

Production responsibility does not grant approval authority.

---

## 11.3 Brand Governance

Brand Governance is responsible for:

- Reviewing assets
- Approving or rejecting assets
- Enforcing Brand Standards
- Controlling publication eligibility
- Managing exceptions where formally applicable

However, assets fundamentally incompatible with Mehkam's Brand System remain prohibited.

---

# 12. Governance Enforcement

Any official Mehkam brand asset that does not comply with this standard may be:

- Rejected
- Returned for revision
- Removed from active distribution
- Retired
- Archived

Asset governance applies regardless of:

- Creator
- Team
- Department
- Production method
- Distribution channel

---

# 13. Core Governance Rules

The following rules are mandatory:

1. **Mehkam Holding owns all official Mehkam brand assets.**
2. **All official assets must follow the centralized naming standard.**
3. **Controlled assets must follow the centralized versioning standard.**
4. **Brand Governance approval is required before publication.**
5. **Assets must follow the defined lifecycle.**
6. **Retired assets must be archived.**
7. **Archived assets must remain distinguishable from active assets.**
8. **Creation does not equal approval.**
9. **Production responsibility does not equal ownership.**
10. **Unapproved assets must not be treated as official brand assets.**

---

# 14. Governance Principle

The Mehkam Brand Asset System follows one fundamental principle:

> **Every official asset must have an owner, an identity, a version, an approval state, and a defined lifecycle.**

This ensures that the Mehkam Brand System remains controlled and scalable as the number of assets, teams, channels, and portfolio companies increases.

# Final Statement

This document establishes the official governance framework for Mehkam Holding brand assets.

It centralizes ownership, standardizes asset identification and versioning, establishes mandatory approval before publication, and defines a controlled lifecycle from production through archival.

The framework is designed to maintain brand integrity while providing a scalable foundation for the growth of Mehkam's visual asset ecosystem.
