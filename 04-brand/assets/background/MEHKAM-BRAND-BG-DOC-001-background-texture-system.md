## Overview

This document defines reusable background and texture assets for Mehkam Holding.

## Principles

- Minimal
- Structured
- Monochromatic
- Low visual noise
- Strong readability
- Consistent with Mehkam typography and logo system

## Asset Families

- Light backgrounds
- Dark backgrounds
- Geometric backgrounds
- Subtle textures
- Presentation backgrounds

## Usage

Backgrounds must support content hierarchy and must never compete with Mehkam typography, logo, or primary communication.

## Restrictions

Avoid bright colors, heavy gradients, excessive patterns, decorative clutter, and backgrounds that reduce text or logo legibility.

## Governance

Approved backgrounds must be registered in the master background and texture manifest before production use.
