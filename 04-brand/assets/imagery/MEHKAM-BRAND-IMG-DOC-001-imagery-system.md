# Mehkam Imagery System

## Overview

This document defines the visual direction, organization, and production requirements for imagery used across Mehkam Holding.

## Visual Principles

- Premium
- Corporate
- Minimal
- Structured
- Professional
- Leadership-oriented
- Monochromatic or low-saturation
- Controlled lighting
- Generous negative space

## Imagery Categories

### People

Leadership, professional collaboration, executive environments, and corporate teams.

### Corporate

Office environments, architecture, meeting spaces, and organizational environments.

### Technology

Software engineering, artificial intelligence, data systems, infrastructure, and innovation.

## Photography Direction

Imagery should feel editorial, intentional, credible, and contemporary. Avoid generic stock-photo expressions, excessive posing, visual clutter, artificial lens effects, and overly saturated palettes.

## AI-Generated Imagery Direction

AI-generated imagery must follow the same composition, lighting, tonal, and organizational principles as photography. Generated imagery must not introduce visual styles that conflict with the Mehkam monochromatic identity.

## Composition

Use strong geometry, clean perspective, controlled depth, negative space, and clear focal hierarchy. Imagery should support typography and logo placement rather than compete with them.

## Prohibited Styles

- Neon or highly saturated palettes
- Generic futuristic clichés
- Excessive 3D effects
- Decorative visual noise
- Uncontrolled gradients
- Distorted or unreadable human anatomy
- Unprofessional corporate stock aesthetics

## Asset Governance

All approved imagery must be stored in the approved category directories and registered in the master imagery manifest before production use.

## Final Statement

The Mehkam Imagery System establishes the visual foundation for people, corporate, technology, and AI-generated imagery across the Mehkam ecosystem.
