# Official Company Profile

## Overview

This document provides the official company profile of Kara Holding.

It consolidates the organization's identity, business model, organizational scope, portfolio structure, and strategic direction into a single authoritative reference for internal and external use.

# 1. Company Summary

Kara Holding is a technology holding company focused on building, operating, and investing in AI-first software companies.

The organization creates scalable technology businesses by combining exceptional engineering, strategic leadership, operational excellence, and long-term innovation.

Kara Holding serves as the parent organization responsible for establishing standards, governance, and shared infrastructure across its entire ecosystem.

# 2. Vision

Build one of the world's most respected ecosystems of AI-first technology companies through engineering excellence, innovation, and sustainable growth.

# 3. Mission

Empower the future by creating intelligent software companies that solve meaningful problems and deliver long-term value for customers, businesses, and society.

# 4. Business Model

Kara Holding develops, operates, and scales independent technology companies.

Each portfolio company benefits from shared organizational capabilities while maintaining operational independence.

Shared capabilities include:

- Engineering
- Product Strategy
- Documentation
- Design Systems
- Brand Governance
- Infrastructure
- Security
- Operations

# 5. Portfolio Companies

## Kara Holding

Parent organization responsible for governance, strategy, engineering standards, and organizational leadership.

## Chasha

Restaurant Operating System designed to modernize restaurant operations through intelligent ordering, operational efficiency, and digital workflows.

## Parifan

AI-powered customer support platform focused on intelligent communication, automation, and scalable customer engagement.

# 6. Organizational Philosophy

Kara Holding follows a centralized governance model with decentralized execution.

Strategic direction, standards, and organizational frameworks are managed by the holding, while portfolio companies independently execute their products, operations, and market strategies.

# 7. Long-Term Objectives

- Build globally competitive software companies.
- Lead AI-first product development.
- Establish world-class engineering standards.
- Create reusable organizational systems.
- Scale sustainable technology businesses.
- Continuously invest in innovation.
