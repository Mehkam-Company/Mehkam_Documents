# Company Information and Organizational Scope

## Overview

This document defines the official company information and organizational scope of Mehkam Holding.

# 2. Organizational Scope

Mehkam Holding serves as the governing organization for all companies operating within the Mehkam ecosystem.

The holding establishes shared standards, strategic direction, engineering governance, operational processes, and organizational policies while enabling portfolio companies to operate independently.

# 3. Governance Scope

Mehkam Holding governs:

- Corporate strategy
- Engineering standards
- Product governance
- Brand governance
- Documentation standards
- Operational policies
- Information security
- Technology strategy
- Investment strategy

# 4. Portfolio Scope

The organizational scope includes:

- Existing portfolio companies
- Future subsidiaries
- Internal technology platforms
- Shared engineering services
- Shared operational services
- Shared design systems
- Shared documentation framework

# 5. Organizational Boundaries

The holding is responsible for defining organizational standards and governance.

Portfolio companies are responsible for:

- Product execution
- Daily operations
- Customer management
- Market expansion
- Product-specific decision making

Strategic alignment with Mehkam Holding standards remains mandatory.

# 6. Scope Limitations

This document defines organizational scope only.

Detailed governance models, legal structures, operational procedures, investment strategies, and engineering standards are documented separately.
