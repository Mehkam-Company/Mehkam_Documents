# Holding Structure and Business Model

## Overview

This document defines the organizational structure and business model of Mehkam Holding.

It explains how the holding company operates, governs portfolio companies, allocates resources, and creates long-term value across the organization.

# 1. Holding Structure

Mehkam Holding operates as a centralized technology holding company.

The holding provides strategic leadership, governance, engineering standards, operational processes, and shared infrastructure for all portfolio companies.

Portfolio companies operate independently while aligning with Mehkam Holding's organizational standards and long-term strategy.

# 2. Organizational Model

The organizational model consists of two primary layers:

## Holding Layer

Responsible for:

- Corporate governance
- Business strategy
- Technology strategy
- Investment management
- Brand governance
- Engineering standards
- Shared operations

## Portfolio Layer

Responsible for:

- Product development
- Market execution
- Customer operations
- Product growth
- Revenue generation

# 3. Business Model

Mehkam Holding creates value by building, operating, and scaling technology companies.

The holding invests in internal ventures and provides centralized capabilities that reduce operational overhead while improving execution quality across the portfolio.

# 4. Shared Capabilities

All portfolio companies may leverage shared resources, including:

- Engineering standards
- Documentation standards
- Design systems
- Brand assets
- Infrastructure
- Security practices
- Operational processes
- Legal guidance

# 5. Portfolio Governance

Each company maintains operational autonomy while following governance policies established by Mehkam Holding.

Strategic decisions, organizational standards, and cross-company initiatives are coordinated by the holding.

# 6. Value Creation Strategy

Mehkam Holding creates long-term value through:

- Building AI-first companies
- Developing scalable software platforms
- Standardizing engineering excellence
- Sharing operational knowledge
- Supporting sustainable growth
- Investing in innovation

# 7. Growth Strategy

The holding expands by:

- Launching new technology companies
- Scaling existing portfolio businesses
- Reusing internal platforms and standards
- Strengthening organizational capabilities
- Investing in emerging technologies

# Final Statement

The holding structure and business model defined in this document establish the operational foundation of Mehkam Holding and guide the relationship between the holding organization and all portfolio companies.
