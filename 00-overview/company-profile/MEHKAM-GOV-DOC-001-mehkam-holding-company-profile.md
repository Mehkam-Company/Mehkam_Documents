# 2. Company Overview

Mehkam Holding is a technology holding company focused on building, operating, and investing in AI-first software companies.

The company develops scalable digital products, creates and grows independent startups, and provides the engineering, operational, strategic, and branding foundation required for sustainable long-term growth.

Rather than operating as a traditional software company, Mehkam Holding functions as a technology ecosystem where multiple companies share common standards, governance, engineering excellence, and operational infrastructure while maintaining product independence.

# 3. Company Purpose

Mehkam Holding exists to create technology companies capable of solving meaningful problems through software, artificial intelligence, and exceptional engineering.

The organization provides the strategic direction, technical leadership, operational excellence, and organizational support required for each portfolio company to achieve sustainable growth.

# 4. Core Business Areas

Mehkam Holding operates across multiple technology domains, including:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Software Engineering
- SaaS Platforms
- Digital Products
- Startup Development
- Product Design
- Technology Infrastructure
- Research and Innovation

# 5. Organizational Scope

Mehkam Holding is responsible for establishing and maintaining:

- Corporate strategy
- Technology strategy
- Engineering standards
- Product governance
- Brand governance
- Operational processes
- Investment management
- Portfolio management
- Organizational development
- Long-term innovation

# 6. Strategic Position

Mehkam Holding acts as the parent organization for all portfolio companies.

Each subsidiary operates independently while leveraging shared infrastructure, engineering standards, governance, documentation, operational processes, and strategic leadership provided by the holding.

This operating model enables consistency, scalability, and sustainable long-term growth across the entire ecosystem.

# 7. Long-Term Vision

Mehkam Holding aims to build a globally recognized ecosystem of technology companies that combine innovation, intelligent software, exceptional engineering, and sustainable business growth.
