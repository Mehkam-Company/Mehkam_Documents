# Future Market Position

## Overview

This document defines the long-term market position Mehkam Holding aims to achieve.

It establishes how the organization intends to be recognized within the global technology ecosystem and provides strategic direction for portfolio development, investment decisions, and organizational growth.

# 1. Market Position

Mehkam Holding is a technology holding company dedicated to building, growing, and operating exceptional technology companies.

Rather than focusing on a single technology, industry, or product category, Mehkam Holding focuses on creating sustainable technology businesses capable of solving meaningful problems and competing on a global scale.

# 2. Strategic Position

Mehkam Holding seeks to become one of the world's most respected technology holding companies by consistently creating successful technology businesses supported by strong engineering, disciplined execution, and long-term strategic thinking.

# 3. Market Identity

Mehkam Holding positions itself as:

- A technology holding company.
- A builder of technology businesses.
- A long-term strategic organization.
- A company-building ecosystem.
- A center of engineering and operational excellence.

# 4. Competitive Differentiation

Mehkam Holding differentiates itself through:

- Long-term company building instead of short-term product development.
- Strong engineering culture.
- Standardized organizational systems.
- Shared operational capabilities across portfolio companies.
- Sustainable growth through disciplined execution.

# 5. Strategic Focus

The organization focuses exclusively on technology companies.

Portfolio companies may operate in different technology sectors, including but not limited to:

- Software
- Artificial Intelligence
- FinTech
- HealthTech
- Developer Tools
- Cloud Computing
- Cybersecurity
- Enterprise Technology
- Future technology markets

The holding itself is technology-focused rather than technology-specific.

# 6. Long-Term Market Ambition

Mehkam Holding aims to become a globally recognized organization known for repeatedly building successful technology companies that create lasting value for customers, industries, and society.
