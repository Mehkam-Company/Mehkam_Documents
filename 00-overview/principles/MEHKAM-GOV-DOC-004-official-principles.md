# Official Principles

## Overview

This document defines the official principles of Mehkam Holding.

These principles establish the foundation for how Mehkam Holding operates, makes decisions, builds companies, develops technology, and grows its ecosystem.

Every company, leader, employee, and future portfolio organization within Mehkam Holding is expected to follow these principles.

# Purpose

The principles exist to:

- Establish a unified organizational philosophy.
- Align business and engineering decisions.
- Create consistency across the Mehkam ecosystem.
- Preserve culture while scaling.
- Support sustainable long-term growth.

# Principle Framework

The Mehkam Holding principles consist of three complementary layers.

## Operating Principles

Operating Principles define how the organization works on a daily basis.

They establish execution standards, organizational behavior, ownership, documentation culture, engineering quality, and sustainable growth.

## Decision-Making Principles

Decision-Making Principles define how important strategic, business, engineering, and organizational decisions are made.

These principles ensure consistency, rational thinking, and alignment with Mehkam's long-term objectives.

## Engineering and Business Mindset

The Engineering and Business Mindset defines how Mehkam thinks about company building.

It aligns engineering excellence with business strategy and ensures both disciplines work together to create sustainable technology companies.

Reference:

- Mehkam-GOV-DOC-003-engineering-and-business-mindset

# Scope

These principles apply to:

- Mehkam Holding
- Every subsidiary company
- Leadership teams
- Engineering teams
- Business teams
- Future companies created within the Mehkam ecosystem

# Governance

The principles are considered permanent organizational standards.

They should evolve only when a change significantly improves the long-term health of the Mehkam ecosystem.

Any modification must follow Mehkam's governance and documentation review process.
