# Operating Principles

## Overview

This document defines the official operating principles of Kara Holding.

These principles establish how Kara operates, makes decisions, builds companies, and executes its long-term strategy.

Every employee, founder, manager, and future portfolio company is expected to follow these principles.

# 1. Purpose

The operating principles exist to:

- Create consistency across the organization.
- Improve long-term decision-making.
- Build a scalable operating culture.
- Align every company with Kara's mission and vision.
- Preserve organizational quality as the ecosystem grows.

# 2. Operating Principles

## 2.1 Think Long-Term

Every important decision should maximize long-term value rather than short-term results.

## 2.2 Build Before Buy

Whenever possible, Kara builds its own capabilities, products, and companies instead of relying on external solutions.

## 2.3 Build Companies, Not Products

Kara exists to create sustainable technology companies.

Products are milestones; companies are the long-term objective.

## 2.4 Engineering Drives Quality

Engineering excellence is the foundation of every successful company within the Kara ecosystem.

Technical quality is a strategic advantage.

## 2.5 Systems Over Individuals

Scalable systems outperform individual effort.

Every important process should eventually become repeatable, documented, and measurable.

## 2.6 Documentation First

Knowledge belongs to the organization.

Important decisions, standards, and processes must always be documented.

## 2.7 Decisions Based on Evidence

Major decisions should rely on validated information, measurable outcomes, and rational analysis rather than assumptions or opinions.

## 2.8 Ownership with Accountability

Every responsibility has a clear owner.

Ownership includes both decision-making authority and accountability for outcomes.

## 2.9 Sustainable Growth

Growth should never compromise organizational quality, engineering standards, or long-term sustainability.

## 2.10 Invest in Our Ecosystem

Kara Holding allocates capital, talent, operational support, and strategic resources to companies created within its own ecosystem.

The holding does not operate as a venture capital firm and does not invest in external startups as part of its core business model.
