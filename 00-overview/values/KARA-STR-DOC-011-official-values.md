# Kara Holding Official Values

## Overview

Kara Holding's values define the principles that guide how the holding company operates, makes decisions, and manages its relationship with subsidiary businesses.

As a parent company, Kara focuses on creating long-term value through ownership, strategic direction, operational support, and responsible governance.

These values represent the foundation of Kara's organizational behavior and decision-making process.

---

# Our Core Values

## 1. Ownership

Kara takes responsibility for the long-term success and growth of its businesses.

Ownership means acting as a responsible parent company, protecting business value, and making decisions with accountability.

---

## 2. Long-Term Thinking

Kara prioritizes sustainable growth and durable value creation over short-term opportunities.

Every strategic decision should consider its future impact on Kara and its subsidiary companies.

---

## 3. Strategic Focus

Kara focuses resources, attention, and capabilities on opportunities that create meaningful long-term value.

Clear priorities and disciplined decision-making enable sustainable growth.

---

## 4. Operational Excellence

Kara believes strong businesses are built through effective systems, processes, and execution.

The holding supports subsidiaries by creating operational standards and improving organizational capabilities.

---

## 5. Trust & Accountability

Kara builds relationships based on transparency, responsibility, and clear ownership.

Every team and subsidiary should understand their responsibilities and commitments.

---

## 6. Autonomy with Alignment

Kara provides strategic direction while allowing subsidiary businesses to operate independently within defined boundaries.

The holding guides the ecosystem without replacing the operational responsibility of each business.

---

# Applying Our Values

Kara's values influence:

- Strategic decisions.
- Subsidiary relationships.
- Leadership behavior.
- Resource allocation.
- Organizational development.

These values ensure that Kara remains a strong and responsible parent company.

---

# Values Across Kara Ecosystem

Every Kara subsidiary is expected to understand and respect these principles while maintaining its own business identity, strategy, and operational model.

Kara provides alignment, governance, and support while subsidiaries execute within their defined responsibilities.

---

# Final Statement

Kara Holding's values represent the foundation of how we build, guide, and grow businesses.

They define our approach to ownership, leadership, and long-term value creation.
