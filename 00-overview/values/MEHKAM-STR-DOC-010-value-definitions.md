# Mehkam Holding Value Definitions

## Overview

This document defines the meaning and practical application of Mehkam Holding's core values.

Mehkam Holding operates as a parent company responsible for guiding, supporting, and developing its subsidiary businesses.

These values define how Mehkam makes decisions, manages ownership responsibilities, and creates long-term value across its business ecosystem.

---

# 1. Ownership

## Definition

Ownership means taking full responsibility for the success, growth, and long-term health of Mehkam and its subsidiary businesses.

Mehkam acts as a responsible owner by actively supporting its businesses while maintaining accountability for strategic outcomes.

## Purpose

Ownership ensures that decisions are made with responsibility, commitment, and a long-term perspective.

## Practical Application

- Take responsibility for business outcomes.
- Make decisions based on company interests.
- Identify problems and create solutions.
- Protect the long-term value of businesses.

## Non-Examples

- Avoiding responsibility.
- Making decisions without considering consequences.
- Treating subsidiary companies as independent without alignment.

---

# 2. Long-Term Thinking

## Definition

Long-Term Thinking means prioritizing sustainable growth and durable value creation over short-term gains.

Mehkam evaluates decisions based on their future impact on the holding and its businesses.

## Purpose

To build businesses that remain valuable and competitive over time.

## Practical Application

- Invest in sustainable foundations.
- Prioritize strategic growth.
- Avoid decisions that create short-term benefits with long-term damage.

## Non-Examples

- Chasing temporary opportunities.
- Sacrificing business quality for immediate results.
- Ignoring future risks.

---

# 3. Strategic Focus

## Definition

Strategic Focus means concentrating resources on the opportunities and decisions that create the highest value for Mehkam's ecosystem.

## Purpose

To ensure that Mehkam's resources, attention, and capabilities are aligned with clear priorities.

## Practical Application

- Define clear strategic directions.
- Prioritize important initiatives.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.
- Allocate resources based on business impact.

## Non-Examples

- Starting initiatives without strategic purpose.
- Spreading resources across too many priorities.
- Making decisions based only on short-term interests.

---

# 4. Operational Excellence

## Definition

Operational Excellence means building strong systems, processes, and execution capabilities across Mehkam and its subsidiary companies.

## Purpose

To create reliable and scalable businesses.

## Practical Application

- Improve operational processes.
- Establish clear standards.
- Build efficient organizational systems.
- Continuously improve execution quality.

## Non-Examples

- Relying on individuals instead of systems.
- Accepting inefficient processes.
- Ignoring operational problems.

---

# 5. Trust and Accountability

## Definition

Trust and Accountability means creating an environment where responsibilities are clear and decisions are made with transparency.

## Purpose

To build strong relationships between Mehkam and its subsidiary businesses.

## Practical Application

- Define clear responsibilities.
- Communicate decisions openly.
- Maintain transparency in reporting.
- Hold teams accountable for commitments.

## Non-Examples

- Hiding important information.
- Avoiding ownership of decisions.
- Creating unclear responsibilities.

---

# 6. Autonomy with Alignment

## Definition

Autonomy with Alignment means subsidiaries have operational independence while remaining aligned with Mehkam's strategic direction.

## Purpose

To balance centralized guidance with decentralized execution.

## Practical Application

- Subsidiaries manage daily operations.
- Mehkam defines strategic boundaries.
- Decisions are made at the appropriate organizational level.
- Businesses maintain their own identity while following shared principles.

## Non-Examples

- Excessive central control.
- Complete independence without alignment.
- Ignoring holding-level strategy.

---

# Final Statement

Mehkam Holding's values define the foundation of how the organization operates as a parent company.

These values guide ownership decisions, subsidiary relationships, leadership behavior, and long-term value creation.
