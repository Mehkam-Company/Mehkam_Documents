# Mehkam Holding Technology Strategy

## Overview

This document defines Mehkam Holding's technology strategy and engineering direction.

Mehkam Engineering exists as a central engineering function responsible for establishing technical standards, architectural principles, and engineering practices across Mehkam's subsidiary businesses.

Mehkam Engineering does not replace subsidiary engineering teams. Instead, it provides guidance, standards, and technical alignment.

---

# 1. Mehkam Engineering Role

Mehkam Engineering is responsible for:

- Defining engineering principles.
- Establishing architecture standards.
- Creating development guidelines.
- Maintaining technical quality standards.
- Supporting technical decision-making.
- Sharing engineering knowledge across businesses.

Mehkam Engineering focuses on building a scalable technology ecosystem.

---

Mehkam Engineering defines standards.

Subsidiary engineering teams execute product development.

---

# 2. Technology Principles

## Scalability

Technology decisions should support future business growth.

Systems should be designed to evolve as businesses expand.

---

## Maintainability

Engineering teams should create systems that are understandable, reliable, and easy to improve.

---

## Simplicity

Technology choices should solve real problems without unnecessary complexity.

---

## Security by Default

Security considerations must be part of every technical decision.

---

## Engineering Excellence

Mehkam promotes high-quality engineering practices across all businesses.

---

# 3. Architecture Standards

Mehkam Engineering defines common architectural expectations:

- Clear system boundaries.
- Scalable architecture patterns.
- Maintainable codebases.
- Documented technical decisions.
- Reliable system design.

Each subsidiary may choose specific technologies based on its requirements while following Mehkam engineering principles.

---

# 4. Development Standards

Mehkam Engineering establishes standards for:

- Code quality.
- Repository structure.
- Documentation.
- Development workflow.
- Testing practices.
- Deployment processes.

These standards create consistency across Mehkam businesses.

---

# 5. Infrastructure Strategy

Mehkam Engineering defines infrastructure principles:

- Reliable deployment processes.
- Secure environments.
- Monitoring standards.
- Operational visibility.
- Infrastructure scalability.

Subsidiary teams manage their systems while following these standards.

---

# 6. Security and Reliability

Technology decisions must consider:

- Data protection.
- System reliability.
- Access control.
- Operational security.
- Business continuity.

Security is considered a shared responsibility across Mehkam ecosystem.

---

# 7. Technical Decision Making

Technical decisions are owned by the closest responsible engineering team.

Mehkam Engineering provides:

- Architectural guidance.
- Technical review.
- Engineering standards.

Subsidiary engineering teams maintain ownership of implementation decisions.

---

# 8. Future Technology Evolution

As Mehkam grows, Engineering may expand into specialized functions:

- Architecture team.
- Platform engineering.
- Infrastructure team.
- Security engineering.
- Developer experience.

Growth of engineering organization will follow business requirements.

---

# Final Statement

Mehkam Technology Strategy establishes a scalable engineering foundation across all Mehkam businesses.

Mehkam Engineering provides direction and standards while subsidiary teams maintain execution ownership.
