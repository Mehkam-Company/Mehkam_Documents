# Mehkam Holding Organizational Structure

## Overview

This document defines the organizational structure of Mehkam Holding.

Mehkam Holding currently operates with a founder-led structure where the Founder maintains ownership responsibility and executive leadership, supported by a CEO responsible for company execution.

As Mehkam grows, additional leadership layers and specialized executive roles may be introduced.

---

# 1. Current Organizational Model

Mehkam currently follows a founder-led organizational model.

Structure:

Founder
|
CEO
|
Subsidiary Companies

---

# 2. Founder Role

The Founder represents the highest strategic authority within Mehkam Holding.

Responsibilities:

- Define company direction.
- Establish long-term strategy.
- Make ownership decisions.
- Define business priorities.
- Guide technology and product direction.
- Maintain company vision and principles.

During the early stage, the Founder also performs multiple executive responsibilities including:

- CTO responsibilities.
- Product leadership responsibilities.
- Financial decision-making.
- Strategic operations.

---

# 3. CEO Role

The CEO is responsible for execution and operational management.

Responsibilities:

- Execute company strategy.
- Manage daily operations.
- Coordinate teams.
- Ensure business objectives are achieved.
- Manage execution priorities.

The CEO operates within the strategic direction defined by the Founder.

---

# 4. Executive Responsibilities

Current executive responsibilities are centralized under the Founder.

Future organizational growth may introduce dedicated roles such as:

- CTO
- CFO
- COO
- CPO
- CMO

These roles will be introduced based on organizational requirements.

---

# 5. Subsidiary Relationship

Mehkam Holding owns and guides subsidiary companies.

Each subsidiary maintains:

- Independent management.
- Independent execution.
- Independent operational decisions.

Mehkam provides:

- Ownership direction.
- Strategic alignment.
- Governance framework.

Mehkam does not manage daily subsidiary operations.

---

# 6. Future Organizational Evolution

As Mehkam grows, the organization will evolve toward a more distributed leadership structure.

Future structure may include:

- Holding executive team.
- Dedicated functional leaders.
- Subsidiary leadership teams.

Organizational changes will be based on business complexity and growth requirements.

---

# Final Statement

Mehkam Holding's organizational structure is designed to support long-term business creation while maintaining clear ownership, leadership responsibility, and operational independence.
