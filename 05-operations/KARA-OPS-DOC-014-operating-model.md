# Kara Holding Operating Model

## Overview

This document defines how Kara Holding operates as a parent company and how it interacts with its subsidiary businesses.

Kara Holding exists to build, own, guide, and support its businesses while allowing each subsidiary to operate independently.

Kara does not manage daily operations of subsidiary companies.

Each business is responsible for its own execution, team management, product development, customers, and operational decisions.

---

# 1. Kara Holding Role

Kara Holding is responsible for:

- Defining long-term direction.
- Managing ownership responsibilities.
- Providing strategic guidance.
- Establishing organizational standards.
- Supporting business growth.
- Evaluating business performance.

Kara focuses on creating an environment where subsidiary companies can grow independently.

---

# 2. Subsidiary Company Responsibilities

Each subsidiary is responsible for:

- Daily business operations.
- Product development.
- Customer relationships.
- Sales and marketing execution.
- Team building.
- Hiring decisions.
- Financial execution within its responsibility.
- Operational decisions.

Subsidiaries operate independently while remaining aligned with Kara's ownership principles.

---

# 3. Decision-Making Model

Kara decisions:

- Ownership structure.
- Long-term strategy.
- Major organizational direction.
- Portfolio-level decisions.
- Holding-level principles and standards.

Subsidiary decisions:

- Product decisions.
- Technical implementation.
- Operational execution.
- Customer decisions.
- Team execution.

Decisions should be made by the closest responsible team to the problem.

---

# 4. Governance Boundary

Kara provides governance without operational control.

Governance includes:

- Strategic alignment.
- Ownership oversight.
- Long-term planning.
- Business health evaluation.

Kara does not:

- Manage daily tasks.
- Replace subsidiary leadership.
- Control operational workflows.
- Make product decisions for subsidiaries.

---

# 5. Operating Philosophy

Kara follows the principle:

"Ownership without unnecessary control."

The holding provides direction, support, and accountability while respecting the independence of each business.

Strong subsidiaries are built through responsible autonomy.

---

# 6. Relationship Between Kara and Subsidiaries

The relationship is based on:

- Trust.
- Clear responsibilities.
- Strategic alignment.
- Long-term partnership.

Kara and its subsidiaries work as a connected ecosystem with independent execution.

---

# Final Statement

Kara Holding operates as a parent company that builds and supports independent businesses.

Its role is not to control operations, but to provide ownership, direction, and a foundation for sustainable growth.
