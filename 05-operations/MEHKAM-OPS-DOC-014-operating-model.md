# Mehkam Holding Operating Model

<div align="center">
  <img src="../04-brand/assets/logo/png/mehkam-symbol-white-2048w.png" alt="Mehkam Logo" width="150" />
</div>

## Overview

This document defines the official operating model of Mehkam Holding and establishes how Mehkam interacts with its subsidiary companies and Business Units.

Mehkam Holding operates as a parent organization responsible for building, owning, guiding, and supporting independent businesses.

The operating model is based on **ownership without unnecessary operational control**.

Mehkam provides strategic direction, governance, organizational standards, shared capabilities, and long-term support while allowing each subsidiary to maintain responsibility for its own execution.

Each Business Unit is accountable for its business performance, team execution, product development, customers, and operational decisions.

# Purpose

The purpose of this Operating Model is to:

- Define the relationship between Mehkam Holding and its subsidiaries.
- Establish clear organizational boundaries.
- Define ownership and execution responsibilities.
- Support decentralized business operations.
- Prevent unnecessary operational control by the Holding.
- Enable subsidiaries to scale independently.
- Maintain strategic alignment across the Mehkam ecosystem.

# Scope

This operating model applies to:

- Mehkam Holding.
- All current subsidiary companies.
- Future portfolio companies.
- Business Unit leadership.
- Holding leadership.
- Shared Mehkam functions.
- Teams operating within the Mehkam ecosystem.

# 1. Operating Philosophy

Mehkam follows the principle:

> **Ownership without unnecessary control.**

Mehkam believes independent businesses perform better when they have clear ownership of execution and sufficient autonomy to make decisions within their responsibilities.

The Holding provides the foundation required for sustainable growth without unnecessarily replacing Business Unit leadership or controlling daily operations.

The operating relationship is based on:

- Ownership.
- Trust.
- Accountability.
- Strategic alignment.
- Responsible autonomy.
- Long-term value creation.

# 2. Mehkam Holding Role

Mehkam Holding is responsible for the activities that require Holding-level ownership or strategic coordination.

These responsibilities include:

- Defining long-term direction.
- Managing ownership responsibilities.
- Establishing organizational standards.
- Providing strategic guidance.
- Supporting business growth.
- Evaluating business performance.
- Protecting long-term ecosystem value.
- Maintaining governance standards.
- Developing shared organizational capabilities.

Mehkam focuses on creating an environment where subsidiary companies can operate and grow independently.

# 3. Subsidiary Company Responsibilities

Each subsidiary is responsible for its own business execution.

Responsibilities include:

- Daily business operations.
- Product development.
- Customer relationships.
- Sales and marketing execution.
- Team building.
- Hiring decisions.
- Financial execution within approved boundaries.
- Operational decision-making.
- Market execution.
- Business growth.

Subsidiaries are accountable for the outcomes generated through their decisions and execution.

# 4. Organizational Independence

Each subsidiary operates as an independent business within the Mehkam ecosystem.

Independence includes authority over:

- Daily operations.
- Product execution.
- Technical implementation.
- Customer management.
- Team execution.
- Operational workflows.
- Market activities.

Independence does not remove the subsidiary's responsibility to:

- Follow Mehkam governance requirements.
- Maintain strategic alignment.
- Protect Holding-owned assets.
- Report required business information.
- Follow applicable organizational standards.

# 5. Decision-Making Model

Mehkam uses a decentralized decision-making model.

Decisions should be made by the closest responsible team to the problem.

## Mehkam-Level Decisions

Mehkam retains authority over:

- Ownership structure.
- Long-term strategic direction.
- Portfolio-level decisions.
- Major organizational direction.
- Holding-level principles and standards.
- Major ownership and legal decisions.
- Significant strategic investments.

## Subsidiary-Level Decisions

Subsidiaries own:

- Product decisions.
- Technical implementation.
- Operational execution.
- Customer decisions.
- Team execution.
- Day-to-day business decisions.
- Market execution.

The Decision-Making Framework defines detailed authority boundaries.

# 6. Governance Boundary

Mehkam provides governance without unnecessary operational control.

Governance responsibilities include:

- Strategic alignment.
- Ownership oversight.
- Long-term planning.
- Business health evaluation.
- Risk visibility.
- Governance-level review.
- Protection of Holding interests.

Mehkam does not normally:

- Manage daily tasks.
- Replace subsidiary leadership.
- Control operational workflows.
- Make routine product decisions.
- Direct individual engineering implementation.
- Manage ordinary customer operations.

Governance exists to protect long-term value rather than create operational bottlenecks.

# 7. Shared Capabilities

Mehkam may provide shared capabilities that improve the performance of subsidiary companies.

These may include:

- Engineering capabilities.
- Infrastructure.
- Security standards.
- Documentation systems.
- Strategic support.
- Operational frameworks.
- Brand capabilities.
- Business development support.
- Organizational knowledge.

Shared capabilities should reduce duplicated effort while preserving Business Unit execution ownership.

# 8. Operating Relationship

The relationship between Mehkam Holding and subsidiaries follows this model:

```text
Mehkam Holding
│
├── Ownership
├── Governance
├── Strategic Direction
├── Shared Capabilities
└── Portfolio Oversight
        │
        ▼
Business Unit
│
├── Independent Execution
├── Product Development
├── Team Management
├── Customer Operations
├── Market Execution
└── Business Growth
```

The Holding provides the ecosystem foundation.

The Business Unit owns execution.

# 9. Business Performance

Each subsidiary is responsible for its own business performance.

Business Units should monitor:

- Revenue.
- Growth.
- Product performance.
- Customer outcomes.
- Operational efficiency.
- Team performance.
- Financial health.
- Major business risks.

Mehkam reviews relevant performance information to evaluate the health of the overall portfolio and provide strategic guidance when necessary.

# 10. Operating Processes

The operating relationship generally follows:

```text
Strategic Direction
        ↓
Business Unit Planning
        ↓
Independent Execution
        ↓
Performance Monitoring
        ↓
Periodic Reporting
        ↓
Mehkam Governance Review
        ↓
Strategic Guidance
        ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

The process is designed to maintain visibility without transferring daily operational control to the Holding.

# 11. Escalation and Support

Business Units may request Mehkam support when:

- A decision exceeds their authority.
- Strategic alignment is unclear.
- Significant risks emerge.
- Major resources are required.
- Holding-level capabilities are needed.
- A portfolio-level decision is involved.

Requesting support does not automatically transfer operational ownership to Mehkam.

The Business Unit remains responsible for execution unless governance explicitly determines otherwise.

# 12. Operating Principles

## 12.1 Ownership

Responsibilities must have clear owners.

## 12.2 Autonomy

Business Units should have sufficient autonomy to execute their responsibilities.

## 12.3 Accountability

Autonomy must be accompanied by accountability for outcomes.

## 12.4 Strategic Alignment

Independent execution must remain aligned with Mehkam's long-term direction.

## 12.5 Minimal Central Control

Holding intervention should occur only when it creates meaningful strategic or organizational value.

## 12.6 Transparency

Business Units must provide sufficient information for effective governance and portfolio oversight.

## 12.7 Long-Term Thinking

Operating decisions should consider sustainable business value rather than short-term optimization alone.

# 13. Standards

All Business Units must:

- Maintain clear organizational ownership.
- Define operational responsibilities.
- Maintain appropriate documentation.
- Follow applicable Mehkam governance standards.
- Maintain business performance visibility.
- Report significant strategic, financial, organizational, and risk matters.
- Protect Mehkam-owned assets and interests.
- Maintain appropriate operational controls.

Business Units may implement their own processes when those processes remain compatible with Mehkam governance requirements.

# 14. Responsibilities

## Mehkam Holding

Responsible for:

- Ownership.
- Governance.
- Strategic direction.
- Portfolio oversight.
- Long-term value protection.
- Shared organizational capabilities.

## Mehkam Leadership

Responsible for:

- Holding-level strategic decisions.
- Governance decisions.
- Portfolio-level evaluation.
- Major resource and ownership decisions.

## Business Unit Leadership

Responsible for:

- Business execution.
- Team management.
- Product execution.
- Operational performance.
- Business growth.
- Risk management.
- Reporting.

## Business Unit Teams

Responsible for:

- Day-to-day execution.
- Product development.
- Customer delivery.
- Operational activities.
- Implementation of approved business decisions.

# 15. Governance

This Operating Model is an official Mehkam Holding organizational framework.

Changes that materially affect the relationship between Mehkam Holding and its subsidiaries require governance review.

Changes to:

- Ownership boundaries.
- Decision authority.
- Organizational independence.
- Holding responsibilities.
- Business Unit responsibilities.

must be documented and approved according to the applicable Mehkam governance process.

# 16. Review

This document should be reviewed:

- When the Mehkam organizational structure changes.
- When a new subsidiary is created.
- When major governance boundaries change.
- When the operating model materially evolves.
- During scheduled organizational reviews.

The Operating Model should remain aligned with Mehkam's Governance Model, Decision-Making Framework, and strategic direction.

# Final Statement

The Mehkam Holding Operating Model establishes how Mehkam operates as a parent organization while enabling independent businesses to execute, grow, and develop within the ecosystem.

The model is built around **clear ownership, responsible autonomy, strategic alignment, and governance without unnecessary operational control**.

Mehkam provides the ownership, direction, standards, and capabilities required for long-term value creation, while each Business Unit remains responsible for execution and business growth.
