# Organizational Structure

<div align="center">
  <img src="../../04-brand/assets/logo/mehkam-symbol.svg" alt="Mehkam Logo" width="150" />
</div>

## Overview

This document defines the official organizational structure of Mehkam Holding and establishes how the Holding, Business Units, shared functions, leadership responsibilities, and operational teams are structured within the Mehkam ecosystem.

Mehkam is structured as a parent holding company that owns and develops independent businesses.

The organizational model is designed to combine:

- Centralized ownership.
- Strategic governance.
- Shared capabilities.
- Decentralized execution.
- Business Unit independence.
- Clear accountability.

The structure is designed to evolve as Mehkam expands its portfolio while preserving clear organizational boundaries.

# Purpose

The purpose of this Organizational Structure is to:

- Define the organizational architecture of Mehkam Holding.
- Establish the relationship between the Holding and Business Units.
- Define major organizational responsibilities.
- Clarify reporting and authority boundaries.
- Support decentralized execution.
- Enable scalable organizational growth.
- Prevent unnecessary organizational complexity.

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Mehkam Holding.
- Holding leadership.
- Mehkam shared functions.
- Current Business Units.
- Future subsidiary companies.
- Business Unit leadership.
- Operational and functional teams.

# 1. Organizational Philosophy

Mehkam follows a **Holding + Independent Business Units** organizational model.

The Holding owns and governs the ecosystem.

Business Units build, operate, and grow individual businesses.

The fundamental principle is:

> **Centralized ownership, decentralized execution.**

The organization should remain as simple as possible while providing sufficient structure for accountability, governance, and scalability.

# 2. Organizational Architecture

The high-level Mehkam organizational structure is:

```text
Mehkam Holding
│
├── Holding Leadership
│
├── Governance & Strategic Functions
│
├── Shared Capabilities
│
└── Business Units
    │
    ├── Business Unit A
    │   ├── Leadership
    │   ├── Product
    │   ├── Engineering
    │   ├── Business
    │   └── Operations
    │
    ├── Business Unit B
    │   ├── Leadership
    │   ├── Product
    │   ├── Engineering
    │   ├── Business
    │   └── Operations
    │
    └── Future Business Units
```

This structure represents the organizational model rather than a fixed headcount structure.

Specific teams may change according to the maturity and requirements of each Business Unit.

# 3. Mehkam Holding

Mehkam Holding is the parent organization of the ecosystem.

The Holding is responsible for:

- Ownership.
- Governance.
- Long-term strategy.
- Portfolio management.
- Strategic investment.
- Organizational standards.
- Shared capabilities.
- Long-term ecosystem development.

Mehkam does not normally manage the daily operations of individual Business Units.

# 4. Holding Leadership

Holding leadership is responsible for decisions that affect the overall Mehkam ecosystem.

Responsibilities include:

- Defining long-term direction.
- Protecting Holding interests.
- Approving major strategic decisions.
- Managing portfolio-level priorities.
- Evaluating Business Unit performance.
- Establishing organizational standards.
- Allocating Holding-level resources.

Holding leadership does not replace Business Unit leadership in operational execution.

# 5. Governance and Strategic Functions

Mehkam may maintain centralized functions that support governance and strategic coordination.

These functions may include:

- Strategy.
- Governance.
- Risk management.
- Investment management.
- Documentation.
- Organizational development.
- Brand governance.

The exact structure of these functions may evolve with organizational maturity.

Their primary purpose is to provide Holding-level capabilities rather than direct operational management.

# 6. Shared Capabilities

Mehkam may provide shared capabilities to Business Units where centralization creates meaningful value.

Potential shared capabilities include:

## Engineering

- Engineering standards.
- Architecture guidance.
- Infrastructure capabilities.
- Security standards.
- Developer experience.
- Technical knowledge sharing.

## Operations

- Operational frameworks.
- Documentation systems.
- Process standards.
- Organizational systems.

## Brand

- Brand standards.
- Brand governance.
- Design systems.
- Communication standards.

## Strategic Support

- Business strategy.
- Market analysis.
- Investment support.
- Portfolio-level planning.

Shared functions support Business Units without unnecessarily taking ownership of their execution.

# 7. Business Units

Each Business Unit represents an independent business owned by Mehkam Holding.

A Business Unit is responsible for:

- Business execution.
- Product development.
- Market execution.
- Customer relationships.
- Team management.
- Financial execution.
- Operational performance.
- Business growth.

Business Units are expected to develop the organizational capabilities required to operate their businesses effectively.

# 8. Business Unit Structure

A mature Business Unit may contain several functional areas:

```text
Business Unit
│
├── Business Leadership
│
├── Product
│
├── Engineering
│
├── Sales & Marketing
│
├── Operations
│
└── Other Required Functions
```

Not every Business Unit is required to maintain all functions.

The structure should reflect:

- Business model.
- Product complexity.
- Market requirements.
- Company maturity.
- Team size.
- Operational needs.

# 9. Business Unit Leadership

Business Unit leadership owns the performance and execution of the business.

Responsibilities include:

- Business strategy execution.
- Product direction.
- Team leadership.
- Operational management.
- Financial performance.
- Customer outcomes.
- Business growth.
- Risk management.

Business Unit leadership remains accountable to Mehkam for matters that fall within Holding-level governance requirements.

# 10. Organizational Independence

Business Units maintain operational independence.

They control:

- Daily operations.
- Product execution.
- Technical implementation.
- Team workflows.
- Customer operations.
- Market execution.
- Internal processes.

Mehkam intervenes when:

- A decision exceeds Business Unit authority.
- A Holding-level interest is affected.
- A major strategic decision requires approval.
- Significant ownership or legal matters are involved.
- Governance requirements require intervention.

# 11. Authority Structure

The authority model follows organizational proximity to the decision.

```text
Mehkam Holding
│
├── Holding-Level Authority
│   ├── Ownership
│   ├── Governance
│   ├── Portfolio Strategy
│   └── Major Strategic Decisions
│
└── Business Unit
    ├── Business Strategy Execution
    ├── Product
    ├── Engineering
    ├── Operations
    └── Market Execution
```

The team closest to the problem should generally own execution.

# 12. Reporting Relationship

The reporting model is designed to maintain visibility without unnecessary centralized control.

```text
Business Unit
      │
      ├── Performance
      ├── Risk
      ├── Strategic Updates
      └── Major Decisions
              │
              ▼
       Mehkam Holding
              │
              ▼
      Governance / Guidance
```

Routine operational activities remain within the Business Unit.

# 13. Organizational Growth Model

Mehkam's organizational structure evolves with portfolio growth.

The expected evolution is:

```text
Centralized Business Creation
        ↓
Initial Business Unit
        ↓
Independent Business Operation
        ↓
Business Unit Scaling
        ↓
Portfolio Expansion
        ↓
Decentralized Business Ecosystem
```

As the portfolio grows:

- Business Unit autonomy increases.
- Holding-level operational involvement decreases.
- Shared capabilities become more valuable.
- Governance becomes more structured.
- Portfolio management becomes more important.

# 14. Organizational Design Principles

## 14.1 Clear Ownership

Every major responsibility must have a clearly defined owner.

## 14.2 Decentralized Execution

Execution should remain close to the teams responsible for outcomes.

## 14.3 Minimal Centralization

Functions should only be centralized when doing so creates meaningful value.

## 14.4 Strategic Alignment

Business Unit independence must remain aligned with Mehkam's long-term strategy.

## 14.5 Accountability

Organizational autonomy must be accompanied by measurable accountability.

## 14.6 Scalability

The organizational structure must support additional businesses without requiring proportional increases in Holding complexity.

## 14.7 Capability Reuse

Capabilities that create value across multiple businesses should be considered for shared development.

# 15. Organizational Standards

All Mehkam organizations must maintain:

- Clearly defined leadership responsibilities.
- Clear decision authority.
- Documented organizational boundaries.
- Appropriate reporting relationships.
- Business Unit accountability.
- Governance compliance.
- Organizational documentation.

Organizational structures may differ between Business Units as long as they remain compatible with Mehkam governance principles.

# 16. Organizational Processes

Organizational changes generally follow:

```text
Organizational Requirement
        ↓
Business / Holding Evaluation
        ↓
Structure Proposal
        ↓
Authority Review
        ↓
Approval
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Documentation Update
        ↓
Review
```

Major organizational changes must be documented.

# 17. Organizational Responsibilities

## Mehkam Holding

Responsible for:

- Ownership.
- Holding-level governance.
- Portfolio structure.
- Strategic direction.
- Major organizational decisions.

## Mehkam Leadership

Responsible for:

- Holding-level authority.
- Strategic decisions.
- Portfolio oversight.
- Governance decisions.

## Business Unit Leadership

Responsible for:

- Business execution.
- Team structure.
- Product execution.
- Operational performance.
- Business growth.

## Functional Teams

Responsible for:

- Execution within their functional domain.
- Delivering agreed outcomes.
- Maintaining applicable standards.
- Reporting operational issues through their leadership structure.

# 18. Governance

This Organizational Structure is an official Mehkam Holding organizational framework.

Changes that materially affect:

- Holding structure.
- Business Unit independence.
- Leadership authority.
- Reporting relationships.
- Shared functions.
- Organizational boundaries.

must follow Mehkam's governance and decision-making processes.

Routine internal team changes may remain under Business Unit authority when they do not materially affect Holding-level responsibilities or governance.

# 19. Review

This document should be reviewed:

- When a new Business Unit is created.
- When a Business Unit is significantly restructured.
- When Holding functions materially change.
- When authority boundaries change.
- When the organizational model evolves.
- During scheduled organizational reviews.

The Organizational Structure should remain synchronized with the actual Mehkam organizational model.

# Standards

The following organizational standards apply across Mehkam:

- Ownership must be clear.
- Authority must be defined.
- Responsibilities must be documented.
- Business Units must maintain operational accountability.
- Holding functions must avoid unnecessary operational control.
- Major organizational changes must be documented.
- Organizational structures must remain scalable.
- Shared functions must have clearly defined responsibilities.

# Final Statement

The Mehkam Organizational Structure establishes a scalable organizational architecture based on centralized ownership, strategic governance, shared capabilities, and decentralized business execution.

The structure allows Mehkam Holding to remain strategically coordinated while enabling each Business Unit to operate with the autonomy required to build, execute, and grow its business.

As Mehkam expands, the organizational structure should evolve toward a stronger decentralized ecosystem where the Holding focuses increasingly on ownership, governance, strategy, and ecosystem-level value creation.
