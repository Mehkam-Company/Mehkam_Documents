# Investment Thesis

## Overview

This document defines the official investment thesis of Mehkam Holding.

Mehkam is not an external investment fund. Mehkam focuses on identifying opportunities, creating businesses, investing internal resources, and building long-term valuable companies under full ownership.

The purpose of this framework is to define how Mehkam creates, owns, develops, and scales businesses within its ecosystem.

# 1. Investment Philosophy

Mehkam follows an internal venture-building model.

The process begins by identifying a valuable business opportunity.

After evaluation and approval, Mehkam invests the required resources and forms the team responsible for building the business.

Core principle:

> Mehkam creates, owns, and scales businesses for long-term value creation.

Process:

\`\`\`text
Idea Identification

↓

Market & Strategic Evaluation

↓

Mehkam Investment Decision

↓

Team Formation

↓

Business Creation

↓

Growth & Scaling
\`\`\`

# 2. Business Creation Model

Mehkam creates businesses through a structured process.

The creation process includes:

- Identifying market opportunities.
- Evaluating business potential.
- Approving investment.
- Building the required team.
- Launching and developing the business.

Mehkam is responsible for providing the foundation required for business creation.

# 3. Ownership Model

All businesses created by Mehkam are exclusively owned by Mehkam Holding.

Ownership principles:

- Mehkam owns 100% of created businesses.
- Business teams operate and grow the companies.
- Business assets remain under Mehkam ownership.
- Ownership is maintained as a long-term strategic asset.

Ownership Structure:

\`\`\`text
Business Opportunity

↓

Mehkam Investment

↓

Business Formation

↓

100% Ownership by Mehkam Holding

↓

Independent Business Operation
\`\`\`

# 4. Business Evaluation Criteria

Before investing in any business opportunity, Mehkam performs comprehensive evaluation.

Evaluation criteria include:

## Market Evaluation

- Market size.
- Growth potential.
- Customer demand.
- Competitive landscape.

## Business Evaluation

- Revenue potential.
- Profitability potential.
- Scalability.
- Business model strength.

## Product Evaluation

- Problem validation.
- Product feasibility.
- Customer value.
- Long-term product potential.

## Team Evaluation

- Required capabilities.
- Execution ability.
- Leadership potential.

## Strategic Evaluation

- Alignment with Mehkam vision.
- Long-term ecosystem value.
- Portfolio impact.

Investment decision requires strong confidence in business success and profitability potential.

Mehkam targets opportunities with a minimum 90% profitability confidence based on internal evaluation.

# 5. Resource Allocation Principles

After investment approval, Mehkam determines required resources.

Resource categories:

- Capital.
- Team resources.
- Technology resources.
- Operational resources.
- Strategic support.

Resource Allocation Model:

\`\`\`text
Business Evaluation

↓

Investment Approval

↓

Mehkam Resource Planning

↓

Resource Allocation

↓

Business Execution

↓

Growth
\`\`\`

Mehkam controls resource allocation to ensure capital and resources create maximum long-term value.

# 6. Business Independence Model

After creation, Business Units operate independently.

Business Units are responsible for:

- Daily operations.
- Product development.
- Market execution.
- Team management.
- Business growth.

Mehkam does not manage daily operations.

Mehkam provides:

- Ownership protection.
- Governance oversight.
- Strategic monitoring.

# 7. Growth and Scaling Principles

Business growth is owned by the Business Unit and its team.

Business responsibilities:

- Increasing revenue.
- Expanding market presence.
- Improving products.
- Building teams.
- Creating sustainable operations.

Mehkam responsibilities:

- Monitoring performance.
- Reviewing strategic alignment.
- Supporting major decisions when required.

Growth Model:

\`\`\`text
Business Operation

↓

Business Growth

↓

Market Expansion

↓

Mehkam Governance Review
\`\`\`

# 8. Long-Term Ownership Strategy

Mehkam follows a permanent ownership strategy.

Mehkam does not build businesses with an exit-oriented approach.

Created businesses are maintained as long-term strategic assets.

Objectives:

- Increase ecosystem value.
- Build sustainable companies.
- Create long-term financial strength.

# 9. Profit Allocation Model

All profits generated by Mehkam businesses are transferred to Mehkam Holding.

Mehkam manages profit allocation based on strategic priorities.

Profit allocation may include:

- Reinvestment into existing businesses.
- Creation of new businesses.
- Expansion of Holding infrastructure.
- Strategic opportunities.

Profit Flow:

\`\`\`text
Business Profit Generation

↓

Profit Transfer to Mehkam Holding

↓

Strategic Allocation

↓

Reinvestment / New Ventures / Growth
\`\`\`

# 10. Investment Principles

## 10.1 Build Before Buying

Mehkam focuses on creating businesses internally.

## 10.2 Full Ownership

Mehkam maintains complete ownership of created businesses.

## 10.3 Strategic Resource Allocation

Resources are allocated based on long-term value creation.

## 10.4 Independent Execution

Business teams own execution and growth.

## 10.5 Permanent Value Creation

Businesses are built as lasting assets of Mehkam Holding.
