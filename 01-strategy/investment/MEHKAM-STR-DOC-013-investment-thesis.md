# Investment Thesis

<div align="center">

</div>

## Overview

This document defines the official investment thesis of Mehkam Holding.

Mehkam is not an external investment fund. Mehkam operates as an internal venture-building and ownership organization focused on identifying opportunities, creating businesses, investing internal resources, and building long-term valuable companies under Mehkam ownership.

The purpose of this framework is to define how Mehkam:

- Identifies business opportunities.
- Evaluates investment opportunities.
- Creates new businesses.
- Allocates internal resources.
- Maintains ownership.
- Develops and scales businesses.
- Allocates generated profits.
- Builds long-term portfolio value.

Mehkam's investment philosophy is based on building businesses as permanent strategic assets rather than pursuing short-term investment returns or exit-oriented outcomes.

# Purpose

The purpose of the Mehkam Investment Thesis is to establish a consistent framework for business creation, investment, ownership, resource allocation, and portfolio development.

This framework exists to:

- Define Mehkam's investment philosophy.
- Establish business creation principles.
- Define ownership expectations.
- Establish business evaluation criteria.
- Guide resource allocation.
- Define the relationship between Mehkam Holding and Business Units.
- Establish long-term ownership principles.
- Define the strategic treatment of business profits.
- Support disciplined portfolio growth.

# 1. Investment Philosophy

Mehkam follows an internal venture-building model.

The process begins with identifying a valuable business opportunity. After evaluation and approval, Mehkam invests the required resources and forms the team responsible for building and operating the business.

The core principle is:

> Mehkam creates, owns, and scales businesses for long-term value creation.

The general investment process is:

```text
Idea Identification
        ↓
Market & Strategic Evaluation
        ↓
Mehkam Investment Decision
        ↓
Team Formation
        ↓
Business Creation
        ↓
Growth & Scaling
```

Investment decisions should be based on strategic alignment, business potential, resource requirements, execution capability, and long-term value creation.

# 2. Business Creation Model

Mehkam creates businesses through a structured venture-building process.

The creation process includes:

- Identifying market opportunities.
- Evaluating business potential.
- Validating the underlying problem.
- Evaluating the business model.
- Approving investment.
- Building the required team.
- Establishing the business foundation.
- Launching and developing the business.

Mehkam is responsible for providing the foundation required for business creation, including strategic direction, resources, technology capabilities, and organizational support where required.

As the business matures, responsibility for execution progressively moves toward the Business Unit.

# 3. Ownership Model

Businesses created by Mehkam are intended to remain under full ownership of Mehkam Holding.

Ownership principles include:

- Mehkam owns 100% of businesses created internally.
- Business teams operate and grow the companies.
- Business assets remain under Mehkam ownership.
- Ownership is treated as a long-term strategic asset.
- Business independence does not imply ownership independence.

The ownership model is:

```text
Business Opportunity
        ↓
Mehkam Investment
        ↓
Business Formation
        ↓
100% Ownership by Mehkam Holding
        ↓
Independent Business Operation
```

Business autonomy exists at the operational level while ownership remains centralized at the holding level.

# 4. Business Evaluation Criteria

Before approving a business opportunity, Mehkam performs a structured evaluation.

The evaluation should consider market potential, business economics, product feasibility, execution capability, and strategic alignment.

## 4.1 Market Evaluation

Evaluation includes:

- Market size.
- Market growth potential.
- Customer demand.
- Customer problem severity.
- Competitive landscape.
- Market accessibility.

## 4.2 Business Evaluation

Evaluation includes:

- Revenue potential.
- Profitability potential.
- Scalability.
- Business model strength.
- Cost structure.
- Long-term economic sustainability.

## 4.3 Product Evaluation

Evaluation includes:

- Problem validation.
- Product feasibility.
- Customer value.
- Product differentiation.
- Technical feasibility.
- Long-term product potential.

## 4.4 Team Evaluation

Evaluation includes:

- Required capabilities.
- Execution ability.
- Leadership potential.
- Technical capability.
- Business capability.
- Ability to operate independently over time.

## 4.5 Strategic Evaluation

Evaluation includes:

- Alignment with Mehkam's vision.
- Alignment with Mehkam's strategy.
- Long-term ecosystem value.
- Portfolio impact.
- Capability synergies.
- Strategic importance.

Investment approval requires strong confidence that the opportunity can become a sustainable and profitable business.

Mehkam targets opportunities with a minimum internal profitability confidence of 90%, based on its internal evaluation methodology.

This threshold represents an internal decision standard rather than a guarantee of future business performance.

# 5. Resource Allocation Principles

After investment approval, Mehkam determines the resources required to create and develop the business.

Resource categories may include:

- Capital.
- Team resources.
- Engineering resources.
- Technology resources.
- Operational resources.
- Strategic support.
- Infrastructure.

The resource allocation process is:

```text
Business Evaluation
        ↓
Investment Approval
        ↓
Mehkam Resource Planning
        ↓
Resource Allocation
        ↓
Business Execution
        ↓
Growth
```

Mehkam allocates resources according to expected strategic value, business requirements, execution capability, and long-term portfolio impact.

Resource allocation should remain disciplined and measurable to ensure that Mehkam's internal capital and capabilities are used efficiently.

# 6. Business Independence Model

Following business creation, Business Units progressively operate independently.

Business Units are responsible for:

- Daily operations.
- Product development.
- Market execution.
- Team management.
- Customer operations.
- Revenue generation.
- Business growth.
- Operational performance.

Mehkam does not manage the daily operations of mature Business Units.

Mehkam provides:

- Ownership protection.
- Governance oversight.
- Strategic monitoring.
- Portfolio-level guidance.
- Support for major strategic decisions when required.

The objective is to establish independent businesses without losing strategic alignment with Mehkam Holding.

# 7. Growth and Scaling Principles

Business growth is primarily owned by the Business Unit and its leadership team.

Business responsibilities include:

- Increasing revenue.
- Expanding market presence.
- Improving products.
- Building capable teams.
- Increasing operational efficiency.
- Creating sustainable operations.
- Developing new growth opportunities.

Mehkam responsibilities include:

- Monitoring performance.
- Reviewing strategic alignment.
- Evaluating major risks.
- Supporting major strategic decisions when required.
- Allocating portfolio-level resources when appropriate.

The growth model is:

```text
Business Operation
        ↓
Business Growth
        ↓
Market Expansion
        ↓
Mehkam Governance Review
```

The Business Unit remains responsible for execution while Mehkam maintains ownership and strategic oversight.

# 8. Long-Term Ownership Strategy

Mehkam follows a permanent ownership strategy.

Mehkam does not primarily build businesses with an exit-oriented investment model.

Businesses created by Mehkam are intended to become long-term strategic assets of the holding company.

The primary objectives are:

- Increase ecosystem value.
- Build sustainable companies.
- Develop durable revenue-generating businesses.
- Strengthen Mehkam's financial position.
- Build organizational and technological capabilities.
- Create long-term enterprise value.

Any future change to ownership structure must be evaluated according to Mehkam's governance and strategic frameworks.

# 9. Profit Allocation Model

Profits generated by Mehkam-owned businesses are transferred to Mehkam Holding according to the applicable financial and operating arrangements.

Mehkam manages the allocation of available profits according to strategic priorities.

Potential allocation areas include:

- Reinvestment into existing businesses.
- Creation of new businesses.
- Expansion of Mehkam infrastructure.
- Development of shared capabilities.
- Strategic opportunities.
- Portfolio-level investments.

The general profit flow is:

```text
Business Profit Generation
        ↓
Profit Transfer to Mehkam Holding
        ↓
Strategic Allocation
        ↓
Reinvestment / New Ventures / Growth
```

Profit allocation should prioritize long-term value creation over short-term distribution.

# 10. Investment Principles

## 10.1 Build Before Buying

Mehkam prioritizes internal business creation and venture building.

External acquisitions may be considered when they provide superior strategic value or capabilities that cannot be efficiently developed internally.

## 10.2 Full Ownership

Mehkam seeks to maintain complete ownership of businesses created internally.

Ownership provides Mehkam with long-term strategic control and enables portfolio-level value creation.

## 10.3 Strategic Resource Allocation

Resources are allocated according to long-term value creation, strategic importance, business potential, and execution requirements.

## 10.4 Independent Execution

Business teams own operational execution and business growth.

Mehkam provides ownership, governance, strategic oversight, and support where required.

## 10.5 Permanent Value Creation

Businesses are built as lasting assets of Mehkam Holding.

The primary objective is to create sustainable businesses capable of generating long-term economic and strategic value.

# Scope

This Investment Thesis applies to:

- Mehkam Holding.
- Businesses created by Mehkam.
- Future subsidiaries.
- Business creation initiatives.
- Internal venture-building activities.
- Investment and resource allocation decisions.
- Portfolio management activities.

The framework should be applied together with Mehkam's governance, strategy, operating, and decision-making documents.

# Governance

The Investment Thesis is an official strategic framework of Mehkam Holding.

Investment decisions, business creation decisions, major resource allocations, and significant portfolio decisions should be evaluated against this framework.

This document should be reviewed when:

- Mehkam's investment philosophy changes.
- The business creation model materially changes.
- Ownership principles change.
- Major portfolio structures change.
- Financial allocation policies change.
- Scheduled strategic reviews take place.

Any material modification must follow Mehkam's governance and documentation review process.

# Final Statement

The Mehkam Investment Thesis defines how Mehkam creates, owns, develops, and scales its ecosystem of businesses.

Through internal venture building, full ownership, disciplined investment, strategic resource allocation, and independent business execution, Mehkam builds sustainable companies designed for permanent value creation.

The objective of the investment model is not simply to increase the number of businesses in the portfolio, but to build strong, independent, and economically valuable companies that collectively strengthen the Mehkam ecosystem and its long-term financial position.
