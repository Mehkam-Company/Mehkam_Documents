# Mehkam Holding Business Model

<div align="center">
  <img src="../../04-brand/assets/logo/mehkam-symbol.svg" alt="Mehkam Logo" width="150" />
</div>

## Overview

This document defines the official business model of Mehkam Holding.

The purpose of this document is to explain how Mehkam creates, owns, supervises, and grows its businesses as a parent holding company.

Mehkam operates as the primary owner and strategic supervisor of its subsidiary businesses. The holding company focuses on long-term value creation, business development, ownership management, and strategic direction while allowing each subsidiary to operate independently.

The business model establishes the foundation for company creation, ownership, subsidiary independence, governance, business lifecycle management, and long-term portfolio development.

---

# 1. Mehkam Holding Model

Mehkam Holding is a parent holding company responsible for building, owning, and supervising multiple independent businesses.

The primary role of Mehkam is not direct market operation, but the creation and development of a strong ecosystem of companies that operate under independent brands.

Mehkam focuses on:

- Creating new businesses from internal opportunities.
- Acquiring promising ideas and transforming them into companies.
- Maintaining ownership of subsidiary businesses.
- Providing strategic direction and governance.
- Ensuring long-term business growth.
- Developing shared organizational capabilities where appropriate.

Mehkam itself is not positioned as a consumer-facing operating brand. Individual businesses created and owned by Mehkam represent the ecosystem within their respective markets.

---

# 2. Company Creation Approach

Mehkam creates businesses through two primary approaches:

1. Internal Business Creation.
2. External Idea Acquisition.

## 2.1 Internal Business Creation

Mehkam identifies opportunities, validates ideas, and creates new businesses internally.

The process includes:

- Market opportunity identification.
- Business concept development.
- Opportunity validation.
- Team formation.
- Product and business development.
- Establishment of an independent subsidiary company.

The created company becomes a separate business unit under Mehkam ownership.

## 2.2 Idea Acquisition

Mehkam can acquire external ideas from individuals or teams that have valuable business concepts.

The acquisition process includes:

- Evaluating the potential of the idea.
- Assessing the market opportunity.
- Assessing strategic alignment with Mehkam.
- Acquiring the idea or related rights.
- Building the required team and resources.
- Transforming the idea into an independent company.

The objective is to convert valuable ideas into scalable businesses.

---

# 3. Ownership Framework

Mehkam maintains 100% ownership of all businesses created or acquired by the holding.

Ownership principles:

- All subsidiary companies belong to Mehkam.
- Strategic ownership remains centralized at the holding level.
- Businesses operate under Mehkam ownership while maintaining operational independence.
- Ownership changes require Mehkam approval.
- Business assets remain part of the Mehkam ecosystem unless otherwise approved.

The ownership model enables Mehkam to build a connected portfolio of companies while protecting long-term strategic control and value.

---

# 4. Subsidiary Independence Model

Each Mehkam subsidiary operates as an independent business entity within the governance framework established by the holding.

Each business maintains:

- Its own leadership structure.
- Its own operational decisions.
- Its own product direction.
- Its own market execution.
- Its own internal team.
- Its own business objectives.

Mehkam does not manage the daily operations of subsidiaries.

The responsibility of each subsidiary team includes:

- Building and improving the product.
- Managing customers.
- Executing business operations.
- Managing market execution.
- Achieving defined business objectives.

This structure allows businesses to move quickly while benefiting from Mehkam ownership, strategic supervision, and organizational support.

---

# 5. Mehkam Governance Role

Mehkam acts as the owner and strategic supervisor of all subsidiary businesses.

Mehkam responsibilities include:

- Defining long-term strategic direction.
- Establishing governance standards.
- Monitoring business performance.
- Reviewing major strategic decisions.
- Protecting organizational values and standards.
- Allocating resources when required.
- Evaluating future growth opportunities.
- Protecting the long-term value of the portfolio.

Mehkam does not replace subsidiary leadership or assume responsibility for daily execution.

The holding provides strategic direction and governance oversight while subsidiaries retain execution ownership.

---

# 6. Business Lifecycle

Every Mehkam business follows a lifecycle designed to support sustainable growth.

## Stage 1: Opportunity Identification

Potential business opportunities are identified through:

- Market research.
- Internal analysis.
- External ideas.
- Strategic opportunities.
- Emerging technology opportunities.

## Stage 2: Validation

The opportunity is evaluated based on:

- Market potential.
- Business viability.
- Growth opportunity.
- Strategic alignment with Mehkam.
- Required resources.
- Long-term value potential.

## Stage 3: Company Creation

After approval:

- Business structure is established.
- Required ownership structure is established.
- Team is formed.
- Product development begins.
- Independent operation starts.

## Stage 4: Growth

The subsidiary focuses on:

- Product improvement.
- Customer acquisition.
- Market expansion.
- Revenue growth.
- Operational improvement.

## Stage 5: Long-Term Development

Successful businesses continue evolving through:

- Expansion.
- New products.
- New market opportunities.
- Strategic investments.
- Organizational development.

---

# 7. Long-Term Portfolio Strategy

Mehkam aims to build a portfolio of independent businesses that create long-term value.

The portfolio strategy focuses on:

- Building sustainable companies.
- Creating businesses with strong market positions.
- Supporting innovation.
- Expanding into valuable industries.
- Developing durable competitive advantages.
- Creating long-term value for the Mehkam ecosystem.

Each subsidiary contributes to the overall Mehkam ecosystem while maintaining its own identity, strategy, and market presence.

---

# 8. Shared Capabilities

Mehkam may provide shared capabilities to subsidiary businesses when doing so creates meaningful organizational or economic value.

Potential shared capabilities include:

- Engineering standards.
- Technology infrastructure.
- Security standards.
- Documentation systems.
- Design systems.
- Operational frameworks.
- Strategic support.
- Organizational knowledge.

Shared capabilities should support subsidiaries without removing their operational independence.

---

# 9. Decision and Authority Boundary

The business model follows a clear separation between ownership, governance, and execution.

### Mehkam Holding

Responsible for:

- Ownership.
- Long-term strategic direction.
- Governance.
- Major strategic decisions.
- Portfolio-level resource allocation.
- Long-term value protection.

### Subsidiary Businesses

Responsible for:

- Daily operations.
- Product execution.
- Customer management.
- Market execution.
- Internal team management.
- Operational performance.

The separation is intended to preserve both strategic control and operational speed.

---

# 10. Business Model Principles

The Mehkam business model is based on the following principles:

## 10.1 Ownership

Mehkam maintains ownership of the businesses it creates or acquires.

## 10.2 Independence

Subsidiaries maintain operational independence within defined governance boundaries.

## 10.3 Strategic Alignment

Every subsidiary should remain aligned with the long-term direction of Mehkam.

## 10.4 Decentralized Execution

Daily execution remains the responsibility of each subsidiary.

## 10.5 Long-Term Value Creation

Business decisions should prioritize sustainable value creation over short-term gains.

## 10.6 Scalable Company Building

The business model should support the creation and management of additional subsidiaries without requiring proportional growth in holding-level operational complexity.

Mehkam Holding operates as a holding company focused on creating, owning, supervising, and developing independent businesses.
