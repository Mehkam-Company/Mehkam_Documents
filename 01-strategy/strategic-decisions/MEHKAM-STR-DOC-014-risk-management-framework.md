# Risk Management Framework

## Overview

This document defines the official risk management framework of Mehkam Holding.

The purpose of this framework is to establish how risks are identified, evaluated, managed, monitored, and reported across Mehkam Holding and its independent Business Units.

Mehkam follows a decentralized risk management approach.

Business Units are responsible for managing their own operational risks, while Mehkam maintains governance oversight and protects the long-term value of the ecosystem.

Core principle:

> Business Units manage their risks. Mehkam ensures governance, visibility, and long-term protection.

# 1. Risk Management Philosophy

Mehkam believes that risk ownership should remain close to the source of the risk.

Each Business Unit is responsible for identifying, evaluating, and managing risks related to its own operations.

Business Units must build internal risk awareness and maintain appropriate mitigation processes.

Mehkam does not directly manage daily operational risks of Business Units.

Mehkam responsibilities:

- Governance oversight.
- Strategic risk visibility.
- Ecosystem protection.
- Long-term value preservation.

# 2. Risk Management Objectives

The objectives of Mehkam Risk Management Framework are:

- Protect Mehkam-owned businesses.
- Reduce unexpected business disruption.
- Improve decision quality.
- Increase organizational awareness.
- Support sustainable growth.
- Maintain long-term business value.

# 3. Risk Ownership Model

Every identified risk must have a clearly assigned Risk Owner.

The Risk Owner must belong to the related Business Unit.

Risk ownership ensures:

- Clear accountability.
- Faster response.
- Better risk visibility.
- Continuous monitoring.

Risk Ownership Model:

\`\`\`text
Risk Identification

↓

Assign Risk Owner

↓

Risk Assessment

↓

Mitigation Plan

↓

Monitoring

↓

Reporting
\`\`\`

## 3.1 Business Unit Responsibilities

Business Units are responsible for:

- Identifying risks within their domain.
- Assigning Risk Owners.
- Evaluating risk impact.
- Creating mitigation plans.
- Monitoring risk status.
- Reporting significant risks.

## 3.2 Risk Owner Responsibilities

Each Risk Owner must:

- Understand the assigned risk.
- Evaluate probability and impact.
- Maintain mitigation actions.
- Track risk changes.
- Report risk status internally.

## 3.3 Mehkam Responsibilities

Mehkam is responsible for:

- Monitoring governance-level risks.
- Reviewing important risk reports.
- Ensuring risk management consistency.
- Protecting Holding-level interests.

# 4. Risk Categories

## 4.1 Strategic Risks

Risks affecting the long-term direction and positioning of a Business Unit.

Examples:

- Incorrect market direction.
- Poor strategic decisions.
- Loss of competitive advantage.
- Failure to achieve business objectives.

## 4.2 Operational Risks

Risks affecting daily business execution.

Examples:

- Process failures.
- Team execution problems.
- Operational inefficiencies.
- Resource limitations.

## 4.3 Financial Risks

Risks affecting financial stability and performance.

Examples:

- Revenue instability.
- Cost increases.
- Incorrect resource allocation.
- Cash flow problems.

## 4.4 Legal and Compliance Risks

Risks related to legal obligations and regulatory requirements.

Examples:

- Contract issues.
- Compliance failures.
- Legal disputes.

## 4.5 Technology Risks

Risks related to technology systems and engineering capabilities.

Examples:

- Security issues.
- System failures.
- Technical debt.
- Infrastructure limitations.

# 5. Risk Assessment Approach

Mehkam uses a structured risk assessment approach based on:

- Probability.
- Impact.

Risk assessment process:

Risk Identification

Probability Assessment

↓

Impact Assessment

Risk Score Calculation

Risk Prioritization

↓

Mitigation Plan
\`\`\`

# 6. Risk Mitigation Principles

Business Units are responsible for creating and executing risk mitigation strategies.

Mitigation approaches:

- Preventive actions.
- Process improvements.
- Technical improvements.
- Resource adjustments.
- Operational changes.

Business Units must:

- Create mitigation plans.
- Assign responsibilities.
- Execute actions.
- Monitor effectiveness.

# 7. Risk Monitoring and Reporting

Each Business Unit must continuously monitor identified risks.

Risk reports should include:

- Risk description.
- Risk owner.
- Probability.
- Impact.
- Mitigation status.
- Future actions.

Reporting model:

\`\`\`text
Risk Monitoring

↓

Business Unit Risk Report

↓

Governance Review

↓

Mehkam Oversight
\`\`\`

# 8. Risk Escalation Model

Business Units manage their own risks.

Significant risks are reported to Mehkam through periodic governance reporting.

Immediate escalation is not required unless defined by future governance policies.

The Mehkam Risk Management Framework establishes a decentralized approach where Business Units own their risks and Mehkam protects the long-term stability of the ecosystem.

Through clear ownership, structured assessment, and governance oversight, Mehkam ensures sustainable growth while maintaining business independence.
