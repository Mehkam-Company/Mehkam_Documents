# Mehkam Portfolio Management Framework

## Overview

This document defines the official portfolio management framework of Mehkam Holding.

The purpose of this framework is to establish how MEHKAM manages, evaluates, and develops its portfolio of businesses.

MEHKAM operates as the parent holding company that creates, acquires, owns, and supervises multiple independent businesses. Every business owned by MEHKAM becomes part of the MEHKAM portfolio and is managed through a strategic portfolio approach.

This framework ensures:

- Clear visibility across all MEHKAM businesses.
- Strategic alignment between subsidiaries and MEHKAM vision.
- Effective business evaluation.
- Long-term portfolio growth.
- Consistent decision-making across the holding ecosystem.

# 1. Portfolio Structure

MEHKAM Portfolio includes all businesses created or acquired by Mehkam Holding.

The portfolio represents the complete ecosystem of companies owned by MEHKAM.

MEHKAM does not operate as a single operating business. Instead, it manages a collection of independent businesses that each focus on their own markets and customers.

The portfolio consists of:

## 1.1 Created Businesses

Businesses internally created by MEHKAM.

These businesses originate from:

- Internal opportunities.
- Market analysis.
- Strategic initiatives.
- New business ideas identified by MEHKAM.

MEHKAM provides the initial foundation required to transform an idea into an independent company.

## 1.2 Acquired Businesses

Businesses or ideas acquired by MEHKAM from external individuals or teams.

The acquisition process allows MEHKAM to identify valuable opportunities and transform them into scalable companies.

Acquired businesses become part of the MEHKAM portfolio after successful evaluation and integration.

## 1.3 Strategic Assets

Strategic assets are resources that support the growth and sustainability of MEHKAM businesses.

Examples include:

- Technology capabilities.
- Operational knowledge.
- Brand assets.
- Internal expertise.
- Business development capabilities.

# 2. Subsidiary Lifecycle Management

Every MEHKAM business follows a structured lifecycle.

The lifecycle allows MEHKAM to provide appropriate support and governance based on each business stage.

## 2.1 Idea Stage

At this stage, a potential opportunity is identified.

Activities include:

- Opportunity discovery.
- Initial research.
- Strategic evaluation.

## 2.2 Validation Stage

The opportunity is evaluated to determine its potential.

Evaluation includes:

- Market opportunity.
- Business feasibility.
- Strategic alignment.
- Growth potential.

## 2.3 Creation Stage

After validation approval, MEHKAM creates a new business entity.

Activities include:

- Building the initial team.
- Developing the business foundation.
- Establishing operational structure.

## 2.4 Growth Stage

The business operates independently and focuses on:

- Product development.
- Customer acquisition.
- Market expansion.
- Revenue growth.

## 2.5 Mature Business Stage

Successful businesses reach operational maturity.

Focus areas include:

- Sustainable growth.
- Market leadership.
- Expansion opportunities.
- Operational optimization.

## 2.6 Expansion / Transformation Stage

Businesses may enter new growth phases through:

- New markets.
- New products.
- Strategic changes.
- Business transformation.

# 3. Business Evaluation Framework

MEHKAM does not apply one fixed evaluation model to all businesses.

Each business is evaluated based on its specific characteristics.

Evaluation criteria depend on:

- Industry.
- Business model.
- Growth stage.
- Strategic importance.
- Market conditions.

Potential evaluation areas include:

## 3.1 Financial Performance

Evaluation of:

- Revenue growth.
- Profitability.
- Financial sustainability.
- Business economics.

## 3.2 Product Development

Evaluation of:

- Product progress.
- Customer value.
- Innovation capability.
- Product-market alignment.

## 3.3 Market Position

Evaluation of:

- Market growth.
- Competitive position.
- Brand strength.
- Customer adoption.

## 3.4 Operational Health

Evaluation of:

- Team effectiveness.
- Operational processes.
- Execution capability.
- Business stability.

## 3.5 Strategic Alignment

Evaluation of:

- Alignment with MEHKAM direction.
- Long-term potential.
- Contribution to portfolio strategy.

## 3.6 Risk Assessment

Evaluation of:

- Business risks.
- Market risks.
- Operational risks.
- Strategic risks.

# 4. Portfolio Review Process

MEHKAM performs portfolio reviews to maintain strategic visibility and ensure long-term alignment.

## 4.1 Regular Review Cycle

Portfolio reviews are conducted:

- Every 6 months.

The review frequency allows MEHKAM to evaluate business progress while maintaining operational independence for subsidiaries.

## 4.2 Additional Reviews

Additional reviews may happen when required by strategic situations.

Examples:

- Major business changes.
- Investment decisions.
- Market opportunities.
- Significant risks.

## 4.3 Review Objectives

Portfolio reviews focus on:

- Understanding business performance.
- Evaluating strategic alignment.
- Identifying growth opportunities.
- Detecting potential risks.
- Making long-term decisions.

# 5. Strategic Alignment Management

MEHKAM ensures that every business remains aligned with the overall holding vision.

Alignment is maintained through:

- Strategic direction.
- Ownership structure.
- Periodic portfolio reviews.
- Governance principles.

Subsidiaries maintain operational independence while MEHKAM maintains ownership responsibility and strategic oversight.

# Final Statement

MEHKAM Portfolio Management Framework provides the foundation for managing a scalable ecosystem of independent businesses.

Through structured lifecycle management, adaptive evaluation methods, and strategic portfolio oversight, MEHKAM ensures that every business can grow independently while contributing to the long-term value of the holding.
